# Phase 15 metric definitions, version 1

This contract is defined before new main experiments. It does not retroactively preregister the Phase 13 pilots. The machine-readable policy is `configs/phase15/metrics.v1.json`. A new integrated batch snapshots its policy and evaluator source hash before the first experiment. Retrospective evaluation is labeled accordingly. Definitions, thresholds and match rules must not be changed silently after inspecting final outcomes.

## Primary metrics

1. **Mission completion / MissionScore:** use the strict binary option for the present route benchmark. Success requires every required task to have an observed completion and all required waypoint indices, no duplicate completions, every actual effect matched to an executed guardian selection, and no runtime error. This is route-execution success, not proof of area coverage, target detection, collision-free physical flight or safe return. A started but incomplete mission scores 0. A preflight failure is `not_started`, with unknown mission score and a retained failed attempt. Components are reported separately: unique required waypoint fraction; actual searched-cell fraction when a declared cell denominator and cell observations exist; verified target fraction when targets and verified observations exist; and safe-return evidence only if the mission defines return requirements. A declared denominator with no initialized observation source remains unavailable; it is not automatically zero. Missing evidence is null with a reason.
2. **Completion time:** mission-complete run clock minus mission-start run clock. Successful times are reported only for verified success. Timeouts retain a null successful time, actual observation endpoint and configured budget. Errors and interruptions are separate statuses, never fast successful completions. State whether the clock was simulated or wall based.
3. **Continuity retention:** MissionScore(degraded) / MissionScore(nominal). Match architecture, scenario/mission, seed, task/failure configuration, budget, policy/model/digest/options, ablations, clock and source implementation; only network settings vary. Require a unique nominal reference. A zero/unknown nominal score or absent/ambiguous reference produces null with a reason. Since strict success is binary, also report diagnostic route-work retention as the ratio of waypoint fractions; do not rename waypoint coverage as searched area. Do not clip ratios above one.
4. **Recovery time:** actual disruption onset to replacement **local ownership acceptance** of each unfinished task owned by the failed actor at onset. Acceptances require a local assignment event whose actor is the owner; peer acknowledgments and central assignment proposals do not qualify. This measures local acceptance, not consensus or authorized motion. Separately report onset to first newly completed waypoint by a replacement actor, excluding repeated work. Missing recovery is censored, not zero. No owned task at onset is not applicable. For partitions without a failed owner, replacement acceptance is not applicable; report productive resumption only after an observed communication-related cancellation of previously active motion of the affected actor. An idle rejection during an auction does not establish interrupted productive work. Independent packet loss has no invented outage timestamp.
5. **Safety:** reported per-actor collision episodes only if an explicit collision observation exists (two actors reporting the same physical contact may produce two actor episodes); sampled separation violations per pair/time and contiguous episodes using the guardian's configured minimum; sampled restricted-zone entries and separately inferred straight-line between-sample crossings; guardian fallback counts/types, emergency recovery subset (landing/safe-layer/return-home), unsafe-proposal evaluations and intervention counts. Geometric/reserve violation reasons identify unsafe evaluations; unverified telemetry rejections are separate. Routine `bounded_motion_segment` modifications are separated from corrective interventions. Sampled zero violations do not certify between-sample or physical safety. Missing sensing is not zero collisions.
6. **Communication overhead:** unique physical send IDs, recipient packet attempts, modeled egress bytes whose transmission has begun by the observation endpoint, attempted bytes, sends per uniquely completed task, retry sends, repeated deliveries of the same original transaction to the same recipient, expired/dropped/pending recipient records. One broadcast may have several recipients. Pending future egress is not counted as transmitted bytes. Recreated semantic transactions cannot be recognized reliably without a stable business ID; transport retry duplicates are explicitly scoped.

## Diagnostics

- Unique waypoint work, total recorded arrivals, repeated waypoint work and **route-work coordination efficiency = unique required waypoint units / total recorded arrivals**. Zero total work yields null. This is not area-search efficiency or a flight-distance efficiency.
- Area searched and redundant cell observations only with a declared search-cell denominator and genuine cell observations. Target reports alone are not verified detections.
- Owner changes, same-owner restarts, conflicting-claim corrections and end-of-run orphaned tasks. Final local task snapshots identify nonfailed, unexpired self-declared owners; this does not imply quorum agreement or executability. Disagreement and multiple owners are reported separately.
- Role changes and A→B→A returns within 30 seconds. Such returns are oscillation candidates; “unnecessary” requires an oracle about mission need and otherwise remains unavailable.
- Expired delivered messages marked as influencing decisions, separately from rejected stale-context/task-version decisions. Neither queued nor merely old messages automatically count as decision influence.
- Invalid structured outputs, successful/failed response latency, real LLM calls, deterministic fallback invocations and separately tagged role fallbacks.
- Provider-reported input/output tokens only. No character-to-token approximation. Missing responses/usage produce incomplete token coverage, not invented consumption. Warmup is separate from mission inference.
- Sampled polyline distance and observed simulated battery-percentage decrease per actor. Battery percentages are not joules or a calibrated aircraft energy model.

## Evidence and units

The evaluator reads immutable Phase 14 run outputs and lossless archives. Observers collect own telemetry, final local task states and provider usage without changing task allocation or safety decisions. Optional future search observations use `SEARCH_CELL_OBSERVED` (cell_id), `TARGET_DETECTED` (target_id, verified=true), and `SAFE_RETURN_VERIFIED` events; denominators/requirements must be declared in the mission before execution. A SENSOR_INITIALIZED event identifies an active area_coverage, target_detection or safe_return observation source, allowing a genuine zero to be distinguished from absent collection. Their presence is not manufactured by the evaluator.

Definitions apply to the current mock runner. A detector, swept-area coverage model, collision sensor, certified landing verification or calibrated energy model is not introduced by calculating metrics. Every unavailable quantity carries its data requirement. Corrupt or inconsistent artifacts are flagged rather than silently converted to zeros.
